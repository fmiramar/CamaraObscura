# Scattering Delay Network legal gate

`ScatteringDelayReverb` is not implemented, documented as available, built, or
distributed by CamaraObscura.

The project specification identifies scattering delay networks as requiring a
written patent review covering relevant claims, territories, filing and expiry
dates, distribution versus use, research versus commercial use, and the
effect—if any—of a clean-room implementation. No qualified legal opinion has
been supplied to this project. A GPL-licensed research implementation does not
by itself resolve patent rights.

This file records a blocked status, not legal advice or legal clearance. The
independent `GeometryReverb` implementation uses explicit image-source early
paths and a conventional FDN late field.
